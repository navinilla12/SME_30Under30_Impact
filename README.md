# SME Media | 30 Under 30 Impact Dashboard

An executive-grade, interactive Streamlit analytics app built for **SME Media** to showcase the **30 Under 30** talent directory, corporate pipeline reach, and LinkedIn audience engagement metrics.

---

## 🚀 Live Interactive Prototype
* **Live App Link:** [Streamlit Cloud Prototype](https://sme30under30impact-qyg9ye2piwkemisawv9wdg.streamlit.app/)
* **Status:** Operational (View-Only Executive Mode)

---

## 📊 Core Features & Capabilities

* **Executive KPI Header:** Real-time summary of featured honorees, participating partner companies, total LinkedIn impressions, reactions, and average engagement rates.
* **Interactive Click-to-Filter Charts:** Built using Plotly with `on_select="rerun"` support. Clicking any bar or pie slice instantly isolates honoree profile cards across the entire application.
* **Corporate Talent Pipeline:** Tracks which partner companies and enterprise leaders (e.g., *Boeing, Tesla, FANUC, GM, Oak Ridge*) generate the largest audience reach.
* **Sector Engagement Benchmarking:** Identifies high-performing manufacturing verticals versus areas requiring future content focus.
* **Expanded Control Sidebar:** Multi-dimensional filtering by Industry Sector, Partner Company, Age Range, Impression Threshold, and Text Search.
